How to use EDGE

1. Find a current sportsbook line. 2. Enter a probability estimate and its source. 3. Compare implied probability with estimated probability and ROI. 4. Save candidate bets, then record actual outcomes. 5. Export your history for analysis.

This initial version supports all listed sports through a common EV engine. Sport-specific predictive models, automated feeds, and out-of-sample calibration are not included yet. Positive estimated EV does not guarantee a winning bet.
